Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

This privacy policy describes how 102244501 Saskatchewan Ltd. ("Better Day Apps", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our mobile applications and this website.

Information we collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or location) through our applications.

However, our apps display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect limited device and ad-related identifiers, including your device's Advertising ID, device model, operating system version, and IP address, for the purpose of serving and measuring relevant advertisements.

How we use information

Information collected through AdMob is used solely to serve advertisements and measure ad performance. We do not use this information to identify individual users.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC) processes the limited identifiers described above under its own privacy policy. We do not share any user data with other third-party services.

Third-party services

Google AdMob's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data retention & deletion

We do not collect personal data, so there is no user data to delete.
