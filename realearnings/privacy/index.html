RealEarnings Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RealEarnings handles information and describes the limited third-party services used by the app.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who We Are

RealEarnings is a mobile application developed and published by Better Day Apps, a trade name of 102244501 Saskatchewan Ltd., based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada.

Information We Collect

RealEarnings is designed primarily as a calculator and record-keeping tool. We do not require users to create an account and we do not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information.

Information entered into the application is stored locally on your device unless otherwise indicated by your operating system or third-party services.

Camera Access

RealEarnings may request permission to access your device's camera to allow you to scan a Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) barcode or to take photos or your vehicle.

Better Day Apps does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit these images.

Photo and File Access

RealEarnings may allow users to select existing photos from their device, such as photographs of a vehicle.

Photos selected by users are used only for functionality requested by the user and are not intentionally stored or collected by Better Day Apps.

Advertising

The free version of RealEarnings may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • Advertising performance and diagnostic information

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

RealEarnings offers optional in-app purchases that allow users to remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, depending on your device and platform. Better Day Apps does not receive or store your payment card information.

Third-Party Services

RealEarnings may use the following third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchases (payment processing)

These providers may collect, use, and retain information according to their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

Better Day Apps does not intentionally collect or sell personal information.

Information may be shared only with the service providers described above when required to provide advertising, purchase processing, or app functionality.

Children's Privacy

RealEarnings is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
